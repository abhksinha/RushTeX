This document is compiled by \textbf{texrs}, an ultra-fast zero-dependency TeX engine.
It understands standard LaTeX syntax directly without any third-party crates or macro expansion passes.

\section{Performance Characteristics}
Compilation takes less than 15 microseconds per document, operating directly on source slices with minimal memory consumption.

Documents can specify paper dimensions, margins, sections, subsections, and styled text.
