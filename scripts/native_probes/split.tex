A split in a numbered equation, its number centred on the rows:
\begin{equation}
\begin{split}
(a+b)^4 &= (a+b)^2 (a+b)^2 \\
        &= (a^2 + 2ab + b^2)(a^2 + 2ab + b^2) \\
        &= a^4 + 4a^3b + 6a^2b^2 + 4ab^3 + b^4
\end{split}
\label{eq:four}
\end{equation}
See \eqref{eq:four} and \eqref{eq:after}. Unnumbered, with a space and a starred break:
\begin{equation*}
\begin{split}
\int_0^1 x^2\,dx &= \left. \frac{x^3}{3} \right|_0^1 \\[4pt]
                 &= \frac{1}{3} \\*
                 &\approx 0.333
\end{split}
\end{equation*}
